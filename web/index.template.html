      How to play

      Guess the NYC neighborhood from clues drawn from NYC Open Data. You get 5 guesses; each wrong guess reveals another clue. There is one daily puzzle, resetting at midnight New York time. Endless mode gives you as many extra puzzles as you like, each with a shareable code; skipping one counts as a loss.

      Wrong guesses are coloured from warm (close to the answer) to cold (far away), with the distance between neighborhood centres shown.

      Neighborhoods are the city's 2020 Neighborhood Tabulation Areas. Clues about "per square kilometre" rankings compare the 197 residential ones.
